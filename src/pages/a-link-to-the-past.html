SEVEN LANGUAGES

Play Hyrule in your language.

The game and its menus are translated, not only the interface. On first launch the port follows your console’s language, and you can change it at any time from the startup home.

MODS

A different hero.

Choose a character skin from the startup home, with a live preview and its author’s credit on the lower screen. Drop more mods on the SD card and they appear the next time you open the menu.

  • Previews and automatic SD discovery
  • Modded quests keep their own saves
  • Skins also work with the randomizer

RANDOMIZER

Never the same quest twice.

Generate a seed on the console itself, with no PC or patched ROM. Every seed keeps its own progress, so your normal game is never touched.

  • Open, Standard, Inverted, Retro and Triforce Hunt
  • Saved seeds you can return to
  • Import exact ALttPR full-spoiler JSON for supported rules

Before you draw your sword.

A few essentials for the journey ahead.

Begin your quest

You will need a homebrew-enabled 3DS and your own clean USA SNES game dump.

Download the port Read the complete setup guide
  1. 01

    Choose how to install

    Install the CIA with FBI, scan the release’s QR code, or launch the 3DSX through Homebrew Launcher. Later updates can be downloaded from Settings → Update.

  2. 02

    Bring the original adventure

    Put a clean, unheadered USA .sfc or .smc in this folder. zelda3.sfc is the recommended filename.

    sdmc:/3ds/Zelda 3DS/
  3. 03

    Let Hyrule take shape

    Press A on first launch to validate the ROM and extract the game assets locally. The download contains no ROM or extracted game assets. If the ROM is missing, the port tells you which SD folder needs it.

  4. 04

    Make room for the music

    Audio needs sdmc:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc. Rosalina’s Dump DSP firmware option creates it from your console.

A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Answers for
the road.

From getting started to mods, seeds and saves.

01

Why does it ask for a ROM?

+

The application is a native port of the Zelda3 engine. It extracts the original artwork, maps, music and other game data from your clean USA ROM on the console. Installing the CIA or 3DSX alone is not enough. Place the ROM in sdmc:/3ds/Zelda 3DS/, then press A when prompted.

02

Does it work on Old and New 3DS?

+

Yes, both generations are supported. Rendering performance depends on your console and the scene. Configurable turbo also works on both: hold ZL or the C-stick on New 3DS, or hold X for one second on Old 3DS. A short tap of X still performs its normal action. Turbo speed goes up to x5, set in Settings → Game.

03

Which display mode should I use?

+

WIDE uses the 400 × 240 upper display. ORIGINAL keeps the 256 × 224 game view, and STRETCH fills the display. WIDE has Standard and Fixed camera options. Choose in Settings → Screen; your preference is saved for that ROM. Some rooms do not contain enough original tiles to fill a wider view.

04

Do I need a translated or randomized ROM?

+

No. Keep one clean USA ROM. Languages are chosen in the startup home, and the randomizer generates its seeds on the console, so no patched ROM is needed.

Gameplay-changing hacks such as Redux do not work by selecting a patched ROM, because this port runs its own engine. Its Kakariko shortcut is available as a Quality of Life option.

05

Will updates keep my saves?

+

Updates retain the port’s saves. Save in-game before updating, keep the same ROM filename and back up the game folder before changing files. Normal games, mods and every randomizer seed keep separate saves.

Importing an emulator’s .srm file is not part of the documented setup. Ask for help before replacing a save; renaming a file is not a supported conversion procedure.

06

How do I return to the startup home?

+

Open Settings and tap the home icon, then confirm. Save first: unsaved progress is lost. Settings → Restart restarts the game and your normal saves remain.

07

How do I add a character skin?

+

Copy its folder into sdmc:/3ds/Zelda 3DS/mods/ and open Mods from the startup home. The port rescans the SD each time, so there is nothing to register. Each skin shows a preview and its author.

08

Can I play an ALttPR seed?

+

Yes, for supported rules. Export the full spoiler JSON from ALttPR, copy it to sdmc:/3ds/Zelda 3DS/randomizer/imports/ and choose Import in the randomizer. Imports accept Open and Standard seeds; Inverted, Retro and Triforce Hunt are available through on-device generation.
