    Install the CIA with FBI, scan the release’s QR code, or launch the 3DSX through Homebrew Launcher.

  2. 02

    Bring the original adventure

    Put a clean, unheadered USA .sfc or .smc in this folder. zelda3.sfc is the recommended filename.

    sdmc:/3ds/Zelda 3DS/
  3. 03

    Let Hyrule take shape

    Press A on first launch to validate the ROM and extract the game assets locally. The download contains no ROM or extracted game assets.

  4. 04

    Make room for the music

    Audio needs sdmc:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc. Rosalina’s Dump DSP firmware option creates it from your console.

A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Answers for
the road.

From getting started to returning to the title screen.

01

Why does it ask for a ROM?

+

The application is a native port of the Zelda3 engine. It extracts the original artwork, maps, music and other game data from your clean USA ROM on the console. Installing the CIA or 3DSX alone is not enough. Place the ROM in sdmc:/3ds/Zelda 3DS/, then press A when prompted.

02

Does it work on Old and New 3DS?

+

Yes, both generations are supported. Rendering performance depends on your console and the scene. Configurable turbo also works on both: hold ZL or the C-stick on New 3DS, or hold X for one second on Old 3DS. A short tap of X still performs its normal action.

03

Which display mode should I use?

+

WIDE uses the 400 × 240 upper display. ORIGINAL keeps the 256 × 224 game view, and STRETCH fills the display. WIDE has Standard and Fixed camera options. Choose in Settings → Screen; your preference is saved for that ROM. Some rooms do not contain enough original tiles to fill a wider view.

04

What about translations, Redux or a randomizer?

+

For a translation, keep both the clean USA ROM and your patched ROM in the game folder. Compatible patches can provide translated text while the clean copy supplies the original assets. Compatibility depends on the patch.

Gameplay-changing hacks such as Redux do not work simply by selecting a patched ROM: this port runs its own engine. A randomizer is planned as a possible future addition; it is not currently available and has no announced date.

05

Will updates keep my saves?

+

Updates retain the port’s saves. Save in-game before updating, keep the same ROM filename and back up the game folder before changing files. Each ROM profile has its own saves and settings.

Importing an emulator’s .srm file is not part of the documented setup. Ask for help before replacing a save; renaming a file is not a supported conversion procedure.

06

How do I return to the title screen?

+

The port resumes the previous session. Open Settings → Restart and choose the ROM again to start from the title screen; your normal saves remain.

If an older build goes black or silent after Restart, update to v3.2 or later. That specific issue was fixed in v3.2.

YOU DON’T HAVE TO GO ALONE

Need a little guidance?

If something looks wrong, press L + R + A while the issue is visible. Wait for DUMP SAVED, then share the complete new folder from sdmc:/3ds/Zelda 3DS/dumps/.
