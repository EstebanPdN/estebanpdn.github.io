Prepare the game files

Use the Dusklight extractor with your own GameCube Twilight Princess ISO or RVZ (USA or PAL revision 0, GZ2E01 or GZ2P01).

Forge 3DS Converter

Download Forge 3DS Converter

  1. Select your disc image

    Open Forge 3DS Converter and select DuskLight. Drag your ISO or RVZ onto the window or click Choose ISO or RVZ to browse for it.

  2. Convert and find the pack

    Wait for Game data verified. Click Show in folder to find zelda.twilight beside your ISO.

  3. Copy to your SD card

    On your 3DS SD card, open 3ds and create a DuskLight folder if needed. Copy the pack to /3ds/DuskLight/zelda.twilight, reinsert the card and launch Dusklight.

Use the Python extractor instead

Download and unzip the script, then place your ISO beside twilight_pack.py and name it twilight.iso. Open a terminal in that folder and run the command for your computer.

Download Python extractor

Copy the resulting pack to /3ds/DuskLight/zelda.twilight on the SD card.

Send a dump

Tap DUMP on the lower screen and wait for DONE. ZIP one completed folder from /3ds/DuskLight/dumps/. Include dump.dpk and send each dump separately.

One ZIP, up to 50 MB.
