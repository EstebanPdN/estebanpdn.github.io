SUPER NINTENDO ENTERTAINMENT SYSTEM

A small cartridge.

Star Fox made its world out of sharp wings, angular cities and wide-open skies. Starwing 3DS brings that Super Nintendo adventure to Nintendo’s handheld through a native adaptation of the original source work.

In 1993, the Super Nintendo took flight in three dimensions. The Super FX chip inside the Star Fox cartridge helped draw the polygonal ships, buildings and landscapes of the Lylat system. Nintendo and Argonaut brought a new kind of world to the 16-bit console.

The mission continues on Nintendo 3DS. Explore the development views, get familiar with the controls and prepare your flight kit.

GETTING READY

Coming soon. There is no public release package yet. These are the setup steps documented by the source project for a compatible build.

  1. 01

    Install the matching CIA.

    When you have a compatible Starwing 3DS build, install its CIA using FBI on a homebrew-enabled console. Follow that build’s own release notes.

  2. 02

    Bring your own clean ROM.

    Create /3ds/Starwing/ on the SD card and add a supported .sfc or .smc file. The project does not include game data.

  3. 03

    Let the flight kit assemble.

    First launch validates the ROM and builds Starfox-Assets.BIN locally. Original starts by default. Choose your source file under Options → ROM Files.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Read the briefing.
Know your Arwing.

Original manual
01

Is a public download available?

+

Not yet. Starwing 3DS is in development, and the public repository has no published release package. Follow the source project or join the community for updates. The collection’s 70% is a development status, not a frame-rate or gameplay-completion claim.

02

Which ROM should I use?

+

Use your own compatible, clean .sfc or .smc file: Star Fox Japan 1.0/1.1, USA 1.0/1.1/1.2, Starwing Europe 1.0/1.1 or Germany 1.0. A clean file is 1,048,576 bytes; a 512-byte copier header is also accepted. Modified or unknown revisions are rejected. The project does not supply ROMs.

03

What happens on the first launch?

+

Put the ROM in sdmc:/3ds/Starwing/. The port checks the revision and constructs Starfox-Assets.BIN locally. A matching existing bundle can be reused. Keep the original ROM and generated files together when you update.

04

Can I play Star Fox EX?

+

Original starts by default. The current source also offers Options → Game Version → Star Fox EX, followed by a restart. EX is not fully supported; it has not received the same development attention. ROM Files selects the source ROM.

05

Does it support Old 3DS, 60 FPS and stereoscopic 3D?

+

These are separate questions. Current hardware work targets New 3DS; Old 3DS qualification is still pending. The original game logic runs near 20 Hz, while presentation has a separate 60 Hz target. That target is not a confirmed sustained frame rate. Slider-based depth is experimental and limited to eligible wide gameplay and training; other screens remain flat.

06

How do I keep my saves and settings?

+

Keep your ROM, asset bundle, EX SRAM and autosaves when upgrading. Optional per-game checkpoints are available; autosave is off by default. Settings live in sdmc:/3ds/Starwing/options.cfg. To restore defaults, close the application and rename that file. Save behavior and system lifecycle checks remain part of development testing.
